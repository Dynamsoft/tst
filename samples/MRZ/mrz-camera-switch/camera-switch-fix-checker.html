Camera Switch Fix Checker

Checks the "video turns square / zoomed after switching back > front > back" issue seen on iPhone 17 + Edge. When a camera switch happens, the SDK sizes the preview box from the camera's reported width/height read during the video "resize" event, but on that device the browser is still reporting the previous camera's dimensions and sends no corrected update once they settle, so the box keeps the wrong shape. This page re-measures and re-fits the preview shortly after each resize event (setTimeout 0) to pick up the settled dimensions.

  1. Tap "Open scanner" and allow camera access.
  2. Use the camera switch button: back > front > back.
  3. Watch whether the preview becomes square / zoomed in after returning to the back camera.
  4. The black badge at the bottom shows fix state and resize diagnostics. Tap it to toggle the fix while scanning, then switch cameras again to compare.
  5. Refresh the page before each new open, so there is no hardware camera cache.
